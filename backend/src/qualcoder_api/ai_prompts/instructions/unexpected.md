Briefly explain what empirical results would be commonly expected based on your own knowledge about the phenomenon in question.

Then look at the actual empirical data provided and pick out relevant aspects which are most surprising and unexpected given your previously outlined expectations.

Write a detailed, well-structured analytical response rather than a short summary.
